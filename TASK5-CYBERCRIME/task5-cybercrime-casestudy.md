CYBERCRIME CASE STUDY: PHISHING ATTACK

Phishing is a type of cybercrime in which attackers attempt to steal sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, or OTPs by pretending to be a trusted 
source. This is commonly done through emails, messages, or fake websites.

In a typical phishing scenario, a student may receive a message claiming to be from a bank or a popular service provider. The message usually creates urgency, such 
as stating that the account will be blocked or suspended unless immediate action is taken. It includes a link that redirects the user to a fake website that closely
resembles the original one. When the user enters their login credentials or personal information, the attacker captures the data.

Students are often targeted because they frequently use digital platforms and may not always verify the authenticity of such messages. The consequences of phishing
attacks can include financial loss, unauthorized access to accounts, identity theft, and misuse of personal information.

This case study highlights the importance of awareness and caution while using the internet. Users should always verify links, avoid sharing confidential 
information, and stay alert to suspicious messages to protect themselves from such cyber threats.
