STAY SAFE ONLINE – PREVENTION CHECKLIST

1. Always verify the authenticity of emails, messages, and links before clicking.
2. Do not share sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, or bank details with anyone.
3. Use strong and unique passwords for different accounts.
4. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
5. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions or important logins.
6. Regularly update your apps and devices to protect against security vulnerabilities.
7. Carefully check website URLs before entering any personal or financial information.
8. For UPI safety: Never approve unknown payment or collect requests.
9. For UPI safety: Do not scan unknown QR codes or share your UPI PIN with anyone.
10. Report cybercrime on: https://cybercrime.gov.in
11. Call helpline number: 1930
