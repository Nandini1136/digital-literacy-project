SOCIAL MEDIA DO’S AND DON’TS

DO’S:
1. Maintain a professional and respectful tone in posts.
2. Share useful, informative, and positive content.
3. Check privacy settings regularly.
4. Think before posting anything online.
5. Use social media to build your skills and network.

DON’TS:
1. Do not share personal or sensitive information publicly.
2. Avoid posting offensive or inappropriate content.
3. Do not engage in online arguments or cyberbullying.
4. Avoid clicking on suspicious links or messages.
5. Do not spread misinformation without verification.
